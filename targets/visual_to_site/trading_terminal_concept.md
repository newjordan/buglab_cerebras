# Trading Terminal Concept Target

Concept image path:

- `assets/concepts/trading-terminal-concept.png`

If the concept image is unavailable, use this fallback text target:

Build a dense professional trading and market analytics dashboard for active equities traders.

Required visual traits:

- dark-but-not-monochrome professional surface
- left watchlist with tickers, prices, and red/green deltas
- central candlestick chart with volume bars
- right order ticket with buy/sell controls, limit/market toggle, quantity input, and risk summary
- top market status strip with session timer and major indices
- bottom news and alerts band
- compact typography, no oversized landing-page hero
- responsive tablet/mobile state that preserves the main workflow

Interaction expectations:

- symbol selection updates header context
- buy/sell toggle visibly changes the ticket state
- alerts/news can be acknowledged or filtered

Visual scoring rubric:

- 1 point: correct major regions
- 1 point: credible trading-specific data density
- 1 point: polished spacing and hierarchy
- 1 point: responsive behavior without overlap
- 1 point: interaction affordances are obvious
